Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

  • No accounts, no names, no e-mail addresses, no phone numbers.
  • Your location is encrypted on your device before it is sent. Our server only stores ciphertext and cannot read your position.

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Shares end when the time you chose runs out or when you tap “Go dark”. Expired shares are deleted from our database after one month.

2. How privIsee works

When you tap Go live and choose a duration (15 minutes up to 24 hours), the following happens:

  • Your device generates a random 256-bit AES key. It is placed in the fragment of your share link (…/s/{shareId}#key=…). Browsers never transmit the fragment to any server, so we never receive this key.
  • Our server creates a share entry and returns a random share ID and a secret “owner token” that only your device knows. The owner token allows only your device to update or stop the share.
  • While sharing is active, your device reads its GPS position, encrypts latitude and longitude with AES-256-GCM and sends only the encrypted result to our server.
  • Anyone who opens your full share link downloads the latest encrypted position about every 4 seconds and decrypts it locally in their browser to show it on a map.

Anyone who has the full share link can see your live location for as long as the share is active. Only share the link with people you trust. You can end the share at any time with Go dark.

3. Data we process

3.1 Data stored on our server

For each share our database stores exactly these fields:

DataWhat it is
Share IDRandom identifier that is part of the share link.
Owner tokenRandom secret that authorizes your device to update or stop the share.
Device IDA random identifier (UUID) generated by the app on your device the first time you share. It is not derived from your device, phone number or any hardware ID, and it contains no personal information. It lets shares started from the same browser/app be grouped together.
Encrypted locationYour latest position as AES-GCM ciphertext plus its initialization vector (IV). Only the latest position is kept; every update overwrites the previous one. No location history is stored. We cannot decrypt it.
TimestampsWhen the share was created, when it expires and when the location was last updated.
StatusWhether the share is active or was stopped.

We do not store your name, e-mail address, phone number, IP address or any unencrypted location in this database.

3.2 Location data on your device

To share your position, privIsee asks for permission to access your device’s location. Your location is used only while a share is active, only to encrypt it and send the ciphertext as described above. If you deny permission, sharing is not possible; viewing a share does not require location access. You can revoke the permission at any time in your browser or device settings.

3.3 Data stored in your browser / app (local storage)

privIsee does not use cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for two entries that never leave your device unless stated:

  • privisee.userId — the random device ID described above (sent to our server when you start a share). It stays until you clear your browser or app data.
  • privisee.activeShare — the details of your currently running share (share ID, owner token, expiry time and the encryption key), so that sharing can continue if you reload the page. It is deleted when you stop sharing or the share has expired.

3.4 Server log files

Like every website, our hosting provider’s web server automatically processes technical data when you access privIsee, for example your IP address, date and time of the request, the requested URL, the HTTP status code, the referrer and the browser/operating system (user agent). Share IDs appear in requested URLs; the decryption key does not, because it is in the link fragment. This data is required to deliver the site and to keep it secure and stable. It is not combined with other data and not used to identify you. Log files are stored by our hosting provider for [30] days and then deleted, unless longer retention is needed to investigate a specific security incident.

4.5 Directions and sharing

On the share page you can request directions to the shared position. On Android this opens your device’s map app via a geo: link; on other devices it opens openstreetmap.org (OpenStreetMap Foundation, St John’s Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom) with the position as destination. 5. Purposes and legal bases

  • YouTube video (section 4.6): your consent by clicking “Load video” (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 25(1) TDDDG). Reload the page to withdraw it for future page views.
  • Local storage: strictly necessary to provide the service you explicitly requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making, and we do not sell it.

6. How long we keep data

  • Encrypted location: only the most recent position is kept; each update overwrites the previous one. Once a share is stopped or expired, no further updates are accepted and the share page shows it as ended.
  • Share records (section 3.1): permanently deleted by an automatic hourly job once they have been expired for more than one month.
  • Local storage (section 3.3): until you stop the share (active share) or clear your browser/app data (device ID).
  • Server log files (section 3.4): see above.

7. Security

All connections use HTTPS (TLS). Locations are end-to-end encrypted with AES-256-GCM using the cryptography built into your browser/operating system (Web Crypto API), with a fresh random IV for every update. Even someone with full access to our database cannot read your position without the key in your share link. Please keep in mind that anyone who obtains your full share link — for example by forwarding — can see your location until the share ends.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future.

Because privIsee has no accounts, we cannot identify you on our own. To exercise your rights regarding a share, please send us the share link or share ID. You can end a share yourself at any time by tapping Go dark, and remove all local data by clearing your browser or app data.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.
