```
Be findable. Not trackable.

privIsee lets you share your live GPS location with the people you choose — without handing your whereabouts to anyone else. Not even to us.

Most location-sharing apps send your exact position to their servers, where it can be stored, analysed or leaked. privIsee works differently: your location is encrypted on your phone before it ever leaves it. Our server only stores ciphertext. Your friends see you. The server sees noise.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick how long you want to be findable: 15 minutes up to 24 hours.
• Tap "Go live". Your phone creates a secret key and a share link.
• Send the link to whoever should find you — via any messenger, mail or SMS.
• They open it in any browser and see your position as a live dot on a map. No app or account needed on their side.
• When time runs out — or when you tap "Go dark" — the link stops working.

WHY IT'S PRIVATE
• End-to-end encrypted: every position update is encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device.
• The key never reaches our server: it lives only in the part of the link after the "#", which browsers never transmit.
• No location history: only your latest encrypted position is kept and each update overwrites the last one.
• No accounts: no name, e-mail or phone number required.
• No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
• Expired shares are deleted automatically.
• Open source: every claim above can be checked in the code.

GREAT FOR
• Meeting friends at a festival, a crowded station or in a new city
• Letting someone know you're on your way home
• Coordinating pick-ups without "where are you now?" messages
• Hikes, runs and rides where someone should be able to find you

SEE IT FOR YOURSELF
While you're live, privIsee shows you exactly what the server received: an unreadable block of ciphertext. A countdown tells you when the link goes dark, and you can stop sharing at any moment.

Good to know: anyone who has your full link can see your location while the share is active — so only send it to people you trust.

privIsee — spoken "privacy".
```

### Age rating

- **Apple:** answer "None" to every content question → **4+**. Apple asks about unrestricted web access; answer **No**, because the app only loads its own pages.
- **Google (IARC questionnaire):** category "Utility, Productivity, Communication or Other". The app has no user-generated content, chat, purchases or violence. **Shares location with other users: Yes**. This usually results in PEGI 3 / USK 0 with a "Shares Location" note.
- The privacy policy says the app is not directed at children under 16. In Play Console → Target audience, choose **18+** (or 16–17 and 18+) so the app is not subject to the Families policy.

### Apple App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Apple has no exemption for end-to-end encrypted data, so the conservative answer is:

| Data type | Collected | Linked to user | Used for tracking | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Location → Precise Location | Yes | No | No | App Functionality |
| Identifiers → Device ID | Yes (random UUID, `privisee.userId`) | No | No | App Functionality |

Everything else: **not collected**. The result is "Data Not Linked to You: Location, Identifiers" and no "Data Used to Track You".

### Google Play Data safety

- **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** Yes.
- **Is all user data encrypted in transit?** Yes (HTTPS/TLS).
- **Can users request data deletion?** Yes. They can end a share with "Go dark" and contact `app@seleven.de`. Records are also deleted automatically one month after expiry.
- **Location → Precise location:** Google exempts end-to-end encrypted data from being declared as *collected*. The safer choice is still to declare it: **Collected: Yes, Shared: No, Processed ephemerally: No, Required: Yes, Purpose: App functionality**. The privacy policy can also be linked as proof.
- **Device or other IDs:** Collected: Yes (random app-generated UUID), Shared: No, Required: Yes, Purpose: App functionality.
- No other data types are collected.

### Location permission declarations

- **iOS `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription`:** "privIsee uses your location only while you are sharing it. Every position is encrypted on your device before it is sent; we cannot read it."
- **Android:** foreground location only (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`). If background location is ever added, Google requires a separate declaration form and a demo video.

### Notes for App Review (Apple) / app access (Google)

```
privIsee needs no login. To test:
1. Tap "Go live" and allow location access.
2. Tap "Copy link" and open the link in Safari (or on a second device). Your position appears as a live dot on the map.
3. Tap "Go dark" to end the share. The link then shows the share as ended.

Locations are encrypted on the device with AES-256-GCM (Web Crypto API). The key is only in the URL fragment after "#", so our server stores only ciphertext. The source code is public: https://github.com/mgrgic/privisee
```

### Export compliance (Apple)

The app uses encryption (AES-256-GCM, HTTPS) through the operating system's standard APIs only, which qualifies for the exemption. Answer: uses encryption **Yes** → only exempt / standard encryption **Yes**. You can also add `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` to `Info.plist`.
